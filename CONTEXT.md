# Articulate

A personal practice tool for answering spoken questions in English clearly and quickly, especially follow-up questions, through live voice conversation with an AI partner.

## Language

### Practice

**Session**:
One sitting of practice: two Rounds and then one Transfer question. It runs about 10 minutes, and the Partner wraps up at 12.
_Avoid_: Lesson, workout, call (a Call is only the live-voice part)

**Round**:
One Main question plus its Follow-ups, the Coaching point, and the Redo.
_Avoid_: Turn, exercise

**Main question**:
The opening question of a Round, answerable without specialist or current-events knowledge. It is drawn from the Question bank, as is the Transfer question.
_Avoid_: Prompt, topic

**Question bank**:
The reviewed pool of Main questions, each belonging to a Scene and tagged by category and difficulty. It records which questions the learner has seen and which they struck out; a Session only draws questions that are neither.

**Follow-up**:
A question from the Partner that responds to what the learner just said. Each Follow-up has one Follow-up type. Every Round includes 2–3 of them.
_Avoid_: Probe, challenge question

**Follow-up type**:
The kind of pressure a Follow-up applies. The types are Why, So what, Counterexample, What would change your mind, Be specific, Have you considered…? (the Partner raises a risk the learner hasn't covered, which practises the course's answer to an unprepared question) and Hostile framing (the Partner restates the learner's point as an attack, which practises the paraphrase-and-reframe opening).

**Partner**:
The AI voice the learner talks to throughout a Session. It asks questions in a meeting-like tone, pushes back, and speaks the Coaching point. It never agrees just to be agreeable.
_Avoid_: Bot, interviewer

**Coaching point**:
The single most important improvement chosen for an Answer, citing the learner's own words.
_Avoid_: Feedback item, tip, score

**Redo**:
The learner's second attempt at the same question right after a Coaching point.
_Avoid_: Retry, re-answer

**Better version**:
A rewrite of the learner's own answer in the course's skeleton (Answer → Detail → So what), keeping their position, facts and wording where possible. It is shown after the Redo for comparison.
_Avoid_: Model answer, sample answer, ideal answer

**Holding phrase**:
A short opener that buys thinking time while already carrying content, such as "My first read is …" or a paraphrase ("So the question is whether …"). It is the intended replacement for fillers.
_Avoid_: Filler, stalling

**Thinking pattern**:
One of three named ways to reach an answer quickly, each with a short skeleton: Answer first (Answer → Detail → So what → Stop), First read (First read → It turns on X → If X flips → My call), and Two options + pick (A or B → My pick → Because → So for you). They follow the learner's course (Paraphrase → ADD, and a first read with a tripwire). The app teaches them explicitly at first and then fades the prompts.
_Avoid_: Framework, template

**Transfer question**:
A new question at the end of a Session that checks whether the Coaching point carries over to unseen material.
_Avoid_: Bonus question

**Structure hint**:
An on-demand, silent, one-line reminder of a Thinking pattern. The learner requests it with a button, and each use is recorded.
_Avoid_: Template, scaffold

### Progress

**Response latency**:
Seconds from the end of the Partner's question to the learner's first word of substance. Holding phrases count as substance; fillers do not.
_Avoid_: Reaction time, delay

**Filler rate**:
Filler words per minute of the learner's speech, measured from a verbatim transcript.

**Answer in the first sentence**:
Whether an Answer's first sentence of substance is the skeleton's Answer step (an optional one-sentence Paraphrase may come before it). Judged by the Coach brain; shown as a count, such as 5 of 7 Answers.

**Speech metrics**:
Response latency, Filler rate, Answer length and Answer in the first sentence, measured after each Session and shown on its summary with a trend over recent Sessions.

**Answer**:
Everything the learner says in response to one question, from the first word until the end of the turn. Answer length is tracked as a trend but not enforced. Not to be confused with the skeleton's Answer step: the one sentence that states the call.
_Avoid_: Response, reply

**Next focus**:
A one-line goal at the end of each Session that the Partner reminds the learner of when the next Session opens.
_Avoid_: Homework, goal

**Baseline**:
The first measurement, made from three short unseen questions and scored on Directness, Structure, and Reasoning.

**Weekly check**:
A repeat of the Baseline measurement using new, unseen questions.
_Avoid_: Test, exam

**Scene**:
The image plus one-line setting shown before a Session, used for atmosphere only and never needed to answer. A Session's three questions all come from one Scene.
It is drawn from a fixed, reusable Scene library.
_Avoid_: Scenario (overloaded), background
